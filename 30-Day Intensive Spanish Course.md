# 30-Day Intensive Spanish Course

This is a two-hour-a-day travel fluency sprint for Mexico, Costa Rica, Spain, and Spanish-speaking communities in Texas. In 30 days, the realistic outcome is conversational travel ability: you can handle familiar situations, keep a conversation moving, and understand the main idea of everyday speech. Full broad fluency takes longer and continues through use.

## The two-hour daily session

- 20 minutes: spaced recall, pronunciation, and shadowing
- 30 minutes: new scene and useful grammar pattern
- 30 minutes: role-play with changing details
- 25 minutes: listening lab (first gist, then details, then shadowing)
- 15 minutes: free speaking, error repair, and review

Use the Viajero app for the scene, voice playback, talk-back role-play, and roadmap. On days with extra energy, repeat the listening lab later in the day.

## The 30 days

1. Order with confidence — restaurant basics
2. Customize an order — preferences and dietary needs
3. Pay and say goodbye — the check, tip, and thanks
4. Find your way around — directions and landmarks
5. Move through the city — taxis, rideshares, and buses
6. Tickets and schedules — stations, platforms, and times
7. Week 1 mission — a full day in a new city
8. Check in smoothly — reservations and rooms
9. Ask for what you need — breakfast, Wi-Fi, and towels
10. Fix a problem — polite complaints and solutions
11. Numbers that matter — prices, dates, and times
12. Make and change plans — appointments and reservations
13. Listen for the gist — natural hotel and street speech
14. Week 2 mission — a two-night hotel stay
15. Introduce yourself — who you are and where you live
16. Ask better questions — follow-ups that keep a conversation going
17. Talk about your day — present-tense routines
18. Tell a travel story — simple past experiences
19. Share preferences — likes, dislikes, and recommendations
20. Make future plans — invitations and near-future plans
21. Week 3 mission — five minutes with a local
22. Get unstuck — repair strategies when you miss a word
23. Understand connected speech — fast everyday phrases
24. Mexico listening lab — common Mexican patterns
25. Costa Rica listening lab — usted, vos, and local rhythm
26. Spain listening lab — vosotros and regional vocabulary
27. Handle an unexpected change — missed trains and changed plans
28. Week 4 mission — a day trip entirely in Spanish
29. Full simulation — restaurant, transport, hotel, and locals
30. Final travel day — listen, decide, respond, and reflect

## Fluency habits

Speak aloud every day, even when studying alone. Do not wait for perfect grammar. When you miss a word, ask **¿Cómo se dice...?**, **¿Puede repetir, por favor?**, or **¿Qué significa...?** Keep a short log of phrases you actually needed; those phrases get recycled into the next day's lesson.
