# Lesson 1: Order a Meal Without Switching to English

**Setting:** A casual restaurant in Mexico City  
**Goal:** Greet the server, order food and a drink, answer a follow-up question, and ask for the check.  
**Grammar tool:** **Quiero...** and **Quisiera...**  
**Core time:** 30 minutes

## 1. Sound warm-up — 3 minutes

Read each line aloud three times. The bold syllable gets the strongest beat.

- BUE-nos DÍ-as — *Good morning*
- BUE-nas TAR-des — *Good afternoon*
- qui-SIE-ra — *I would like*
- por fa-VOR — *please*
- la CUEN-ta — *the check*

Keep Spanish vowels clean and short: **a** as in *father*, **e** as in *they*, **i** as in *machine*, **o** as in *go*, **u** as in *flute*.

## 2. Scene — 5 minutes

Cover the English side if you can.

| Spanish | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Mesero: Buenas tardes. ¿Qué le gustaría? | Server: Good afternoon. What would you like? |
| Tú: Quisiera los tacos al pastor, por favor. | You: I'd like the tacos al pastor, please. |
| Mesero: ¿Para tomar? | To drink? |
| Tú: Agua sin gas, por favor. | Still water, please. |
| Mesero: ¿Algo más? | Anything else? |
| Tú: No, gracias. Eso es todo. | No, thank you. That's all. |
| Tú, después: Disculpe, ¿me trae la cuenta, por favor? | Excuse me, could you bring me the check, please? |

Say the full dialogue aloud twice. On the third pass, change the food and drink.

## 3. Pattern — 5 minutes

**Quiero + thing** means “I want...” It is correct and direct.

- Quiero un café. — I want a coffee.

**Quisiera + thing** means “I would like...” It is softer and useful with restaurant and hotel staff.

- Quisiera un café. — I would like a coffee.

Add **por favor** for a natural polite request. You do not need an English-style “Can I get...?” construction.

Build these aloud:

1. I would like the chicken. → **Quisiera el pollo.**
2. I would like a coffee with milk. → **Quisiera un café con leche.**
3. I want water without ice. → **Quiero agua sin hielo.**
4. I would like the check. → **Quisiera la cuenta.**

Visual formula:

> 👤 **Quisiera** + 🍽️ **item** + 🙏 **por favor**

## 4. Speak — 7 minutes

Do not write first. Say each response aloud within three seconds.

You are choosing from this menu:

| Food | Drinks | Useful changes |
|---|---|---|
| tacos al pastor | agua con gas | con = with |
| pollo con arroz | agua sin gas | sin = without |
| sopa de verduras | café con leche | para llevar = to go |
| ensalada | té | aquí = here |

Respond in Spanish:

1. The server asks, **¿Qué le gustaría?** Order chicken with rice.
2. The server asks, **¿Para tomar?** Order still water.
3. Ask for coffee with milk.
4. Order soup to go.
5. Say, “That's all, thank you.”
6. Get the server's attention and ask for the check.

Now repeat the scene with your real favorite meal. If you do not know a food word, describe it with words you have or ask: **¿Cómo se dice ___ en español?**

## 5. Listening ladder — 7 minutes

Have your conversation partner read each version aloud while you look away. After each one, say what the customer orders. If you are studying alone, record the lines on your phone, wait a minute, and play them back without looking.

### Round 1: Clear

— ¿Qué desea?  
— Quisiera dos tacos y un agua, por favor.

### Round 2: Natural

— Buenas, ¿ya sabe qué va a pedir?  
— Sí, para mí los tacos al pastor y un agua sin gas.

### Round 3: More information

— ¿Qué le traigo?  
— La sopa de verduras y un café con leche. Ah, y la sopa sin queso, por favor.

Answers: (1) two tacos and water; (2) tacos al pastor and still water; (3) vegetable soup without cheese and coffee with milk.

Notice that servers may ask the same basic question in several ways. Listen for the situation and key words instead of translating every word.

## 6. Recall challenge — 3 minutes

Close the lesson and say these ideas in Spanish:

1. Good afternoon.
2. I would like...
3. To drink?
4. That's all, thank you.
5. Could you bring me the check, please?

Open the lesson and check. Repeat only the lines you missed.

## Optional second session — 30 minutes

- Record the restaurant dialogue with your own order.
- Play it twice while walking or doing a routine task.
- Shadow the recording twice, matching rhythm rather than reading.
- Retell the exchange without the script.
- Make three changes: a different drink, an item without an ingredient, and an order to go.

## Live checkpoint

Send your answers to the six prompts in section 4. Then complete a short restaurant role-play without seeing the server's questions in advance. Your mistakes will determine what gets repeated in Lesson 2.
